## 2024-05-18 - CRM Pipeline Render Optimization
**Learning:** The `PipelineBoard` component (`src/features/crm/components/pipeline-board.tsx`) previously executed an O(N*M) inline filter on every render (`leads.filter` inside `stages.map`), which is a noticeable bottleneck when dragging/dropping or frequently updating leads across stages. Replacing inline array filters within loops with O(1) `Map` lookups inside a `useMemo` hook is a highly effective, safe, and recurring pattern to eliminate repeated computations in the feature-driven CRM components of this application.
**Action:** Always inspect array `.map()` render loops for nested `.filter()` or `.find()` calls against large collections (like leads or contacts), and proactively replace them with memoized `Map` indexing structures.
